The Collection

Pieces made to be worn, not stored.

Rings, chains, and earrings in 14k and 18k gold, set with stones we hand-select at the bench. New pieces are added in small batches as they're finished, so what you see today may not be here next week.

The Craft

Every stone is set by hand, under a loupe, by someone who will remember setting it.

We cast in-house, polish by hand, and check every prong twice before a piece ships. No two settings ever come out quite identical, and we've stopped trying to make them.
